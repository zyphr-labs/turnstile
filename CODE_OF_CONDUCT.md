# Code of conduct

Turnstile welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Ask questions, disagree with technical decisions, and criticize code. Keep criticism about the work.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual harassment, sharing someone's private information without permission, and repeated personal attacks are not acceptable. Respect requests to stop unwanted contact. Do not use project channels to promote abuse of other people or their systems.

These rules apply to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces. They also apply when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer through a contact method listed on [Hari's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Mr-Neutr0n). If no suitable contact method is listed, open an issue asking for a private contact channel without identifying people or describing the incident publicly. Use GitHub's platform reporting tools for immediate abuse on GitHub.

Security vulnerabilities have a separate [private reporting process](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove content, close a conversation, issue a warning, or restrict participation based on the severity and pattern of behavior. Reports will be handled with discretion, but absolute confidentiality cannot be promised. Decisions should explain which behavior violated these rules without exposing unnecessary personal details.
